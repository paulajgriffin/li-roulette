Where do I get that file?
  1. On LinkedIn, open Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data.
  2. Choose Connections (the fast option) and request the archive.
  3. When LinkedIn emails you, download and unzip it, then drop Connections.csv here.
